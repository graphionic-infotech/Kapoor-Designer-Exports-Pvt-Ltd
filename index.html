ABOUT US

A Surat ethnic-wear house for shoppers, boutiques & resellers

Kapoor Designer Exports Pvt Ltd (Kapoor & Sons) is a women's ethnic-wear business based on Ring Road, Surat — the city's textile wholesale hub. We manufacture, wholesale, export and retail designer sarees, suits, lehengas, gowns, kurtis and dress material.

Shoppers visit our Millennium Market store for wedding and festive outfits at retail prices, while boutiques and resellers across India and abroad order wholesale — with worldwide shipping available.
